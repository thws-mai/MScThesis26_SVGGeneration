\caption{Method / Model Overview}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value} \\ \hline
Architecture & Two-stage pipeline: Stage A: Neural Path Representation Learning (Dual-branch VAE). Stage B: Text-driven Neural Path Optimization(Optimization with Variational Score Distillation and Layer-wise Vectorization). \\ \hline
Stage A & VAE trained on FIGR-8-SVG dataset (200,000 vector icons).  
Step 1: Start with vector paths from the dataset (control points for each path).  
Step 2: Rasterize each path into an image to capture its visual appearance.  
Step 3: Encode vectors (sequence branch) and raster images (image branch) into latent space.  
Step 4: Share a common latent vector z between both branches.  
Step 5: Decode back to reconstruct both the original vector paths and the rendered images.  
Each path is encoded independently; no fixed number of paths. \\ \hline
Stage B & Text-driven optimization aligns latent vectors z$_1$,...,z$_m$ to text prompt via Variational Score Distillation (VSD), followed by layer-wise optimization and path refinement. \\ \hline
Vector Representation & Each path represented by $\theta_i=(z_i, C_i, Tr_i)$ including latent code, color, transformation. \\ \hline
Loss Function & VAE training: $L = \lambda_{cfr} L_{cfr} + \lambda_{img} L_{img} + \lambda_{kl} L_{kl}$. Stage 1: VSD loss. Stage 2: Layer-wise loss $L_{lyr} = L_{CLIP} + \lambda_{IoU} L_{IoU}$. \\ \hline
Training Details & VAE training: Adam optimizer, LR=0.001, linear warmup/decay, dropout 0.1, trained for 100 epochs. \\ \hline
Optimization Techniques & VSD(diffusion model as guidance, updates these z vectors so that, when decoded into SVG paths, the resulting image matches the text prompt), layer-wise vectorization(ensures that paths don’t overlap badly and follow a clear hierarchical structure), path simplification(removes unnecessary paths, low-opacity paths, and merges overlapping paths), CLIP/diffusion guidance(provides text-image similarity score). \\ \hline
Extensions & Supports line art (fine-tuned on sketches), exemplar-based SVG customization, and animation via text-guided optimization. \\ \hline

    \chapter{NeuralSVG}

    \begin{table}[h!]
    \centering
    \caption{Metadata}

    \hline

    Title & NeuralSVG: Converting Text to Vector Graphics via Joint Shape and Color Optimization \\ \hline
    Authors & Bowen Zhang, Zixuan Lu, Li-Yi Wei, Fang-Lue Zhang \\ \hline
    Year & 2025 \\ \hline

    \begin{table}[h!]
    \centering
    \caption{Method / Model Overview}
    \begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value} \\ \hline

    Vector Representation &
    SVG is modeled as $N$ Bézier curves; each curve has 12 control-point coordinates + 1 color vector.  
    You can visualize this as a 2D diamond composed of 4 Bézier segments (12 total points).  
    Each shape also has a scalar index used for positional encoding. \\ \hline

    Positional Encoding &
    Shape index $i$ is mapped to a high-dimensional embedding using Random Fourier Features  
    (64 frequencies → 128D vector, sin/cos pairs).  
    This allows the system to encode periodic geometric patterns across shapes. \\ \hline

    Network Architecture &
    Two MLPs are used:  
    • \textbf{MLP\textsubscript{pos}}$(v_i)$ → $\mathbb{R}^{12\times 2}$ outputs the Bézier control points.  
    • \textbf{MLP\textsubscript{c}}$(v_i)$ → $[0,1]^3$ outputs RGB color (sigmoid).  
    The input $v_i$ is the 128D Fourier feature embedding. \\ \hline

    Initialization Pipeline &
    1. Text prompt.  
    2. Stable Diffusion generates a raster image.  
    3. Attention map detects important regions.  
    4. Sample coarse shapes.  
    5. Convert them into convex polygons / Bézier curves.  
    6. Extract initial colors from raster pixels → $(p_t^{init}, c_t^{init})$ for each shape.  
    7. Train MLPs with L2 losses so they start from a reasonable SVG guess.  
    This avoids chaotic optimization at the start. \\ \hline

    Initialization Loss &
    $L_{pos}(i) = \| \text{MLP}_{pos}(v_i) - p_t^{init} \|_2^2$  
    $L_{c}(i) = \| \text{MLP}_{c}(v_i) - c_t^{init} \|_2^2$ \\ \hline

    Training / Optimization &
    1. MLPs reconstruct initial SVG.  
    2. SVG is rasterized differentiably.  
    3. Score Distillation Sampling (SDS) compares rasterized SVG with diffusion-model output.  
    4. Diffusion model predicts noise.  
    5. Noise gives gradient signal.  
    6. Backpropagation updates MLP\textsubscript{pos} and MLP\textsubscript{c}. \\ \hline

    SDS Text Alignment &
    SDS uses Stable Diffusion to push the SVG toward the semantics of the text prompt.  
    Unlike NPR, there is no latent VAE; gradients directly update the SVG primitives. \\ \hline

    Ordered Representation &
    A nested dropout technique randomly drops shapes during training, ensuring that:  
    • important shapes become stable and survive,  
    • unimportant shapes fade away,  
    • scene is simplified and layers become meaningful. \\ \hline

    Differentiable Rendering &
    The neural renderer (MLPs) outputs colors at sampled pixel locations, enabling continuous rasterization and end-to-end optimization. \\ \hline

    Extra Controls &
    Supports color palette constraints and aspect-ratio constraints. \\ \hline

    \end{tabular}
    \end{table}

\begin{table}[h!]

\caption{Problem \& Task}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Case} & \textbf{Notes} \\ \hline
Task &
Image-to-Vector conversion via layer-wise SVG construction. \\ \hline
Input modality &
Raster image. \\ \hline
Output &
SVG composed of multiple ordered, closed Bézier paths with solid fill colors. \\ \hline
Domain &
Vector graphics, digital illustration, graphic design, iconography. \\ \hline
Motivation &
Existing learning-based and segmentation-based vectorization methods produce redundant paths, poor layering or unstable geometry. LIVE aims to recover a clean, layered SVG structure closer to human-designed vector graphics. \\ \hline
Goal &
Iteratively construct an SVG by identifying missing regions between a rendered SVG and the target image, adding and refining paths until reconstruction quality converges. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

% ===============================
% Method / Model Overview
% ===============================
\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Method / Model Overview}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value} \\ \hline

Core Paradigm &
Iterative optimization-based vectorization. \\ \hline

Initialization &
Start from an empty SVG (white canvas). The current SVG is rasterized and compared to the target image to compute a residual image indicating missing content. \\ \hline

Residual Analysis &
Pixel-wise residual is computed between the rendered SVG and the target raster image. Connected components in the residual identify regions where vector paths are missing. \\ \hline

Path Initialization &
For the largest missing residual component, a new closed Bézier path is initialized (typically as a circle) at the region centroid with an estimated color. \\ \hline

Optimization Loop &
All existing paths are jointly optimized using gradient-based optimization to minimize reconstruction loss. This loop is repeated, progressively adding paths from coarse to fine. \\ \hline

Path Simplification &
After optimization, each path is temporarily removed and the SVG is re-rendered. If the increase in loss is negligible, the path is permanently deleted to reduce redundancy. \\ \hline

Vector Representation &
Explicit continuous cubic Bézier curves with control points and solid fill colors; no latent or learned path encoding. \\ \hline

Differentiable Rendering &
Uses differentiable rasterization to enable gradient-based optimization of Bézier control points and colors. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

% ===============================
% Loss Function (Important: MSE Discussion)
% ===============================
\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Loss Function and Optimization}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Details} \\ \hline

Reconstruction Loss &
The paper explicitly avoids naive Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss, as MSE encourages color averaging and leads to blurry edges and inaccurate vector boundaries. \\ \hline

UDF Loss &
Unsigned Distance Field (UDF) loss focuses supervision near object boundaries, providing more informative gradients for path geometry refinement than pixel-wise MSE. \\ \hline

Self-Intersection Penalty &
Xing loss penalizes self-intersecting Bézier curves, ensuring valid, visually clean paths. \\ \hline

Total Loss &
$L = L_{UDF} + \lambda L_{Xing}$ \\ \hline

Optimizer &
Adam optimizer with progressive scheduling during path addition. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Method / Model Overview}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Value} \\ \hline

Core Paradigm &
Learning-based generative modeling using a Variational Autoencoder (VAE). \\ \hline

Overall Structure &
Hierarchical generative model reflecting SVG structure: SVG → paths → drawing commands → numeric arguments. \\ \hline

Encoder Design &
Two-stage transformer encoder. The first stage encodes each path independently, while the second stage aggregates path embeddings to produce a global SVG latent representation. \\ \hline

Decoder Design &
Two-stage transformer decoder. The first stage predicts path-level representations, while the second stage predicts drawing commands and arguments for each path. \\ \hline

Prediction Strategy &
Feed-forward (non-autoregressive) prediction of all commands within a path in parallel. \\ \hline

Vector Representation &
Explicit SVG paths defined by ordered drawing commands (move, line, curve, close) and fixed-length numeric arguments. \\ \hline

Learning Space &
Vector-native learning directly in SVG parameter space without rasterization. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

% ===============================
% SVG Representation and Embedding
% ===============================
\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{SVG Representation and Embedding}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Details} \\ \hline

SVG Decomposition &
An SVG is represented as a set of paths, each with visibility and fill attributes and a sequence of drawing commands. \\ \hline

Command Representation &
Each command consists of a command type (e.g., move, line, cubic Bézier) and a fixed-length vector of numeric arguments. \\ \hline

Numeric Arguments &
Arguments encode control points and endpoints using a fixed-length vector: $(q_{x1}, q_{y1}, q_{x2}, q_{y2}, x_2, y_2)$. \\ \hline

Command Embedding &
Each command is embedded by summing three components: command-type embedding, coordinate embedding, and positional (index) embedding. \\ \hline

Purpose of Embedding &
Allows the transformer to reason jointly about command semantics, geometry, and ordering within a path. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

% ===============================
% Training Objective
% ===============================
\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Training Objective}
\begin{tabular}{|l|p{10cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Details} \\ \hline

Supervision Signal &
Ground-truth SVG paths and commands from a curated icon dataset. \\ \hline

Loss Components &
Cross-entropy losses on path visibility, fill type, command type, and discretized command arguments. \\ \hline

Path Assignment &
Since paths are unordered, predicted paths are matched to ground-truth paths using an ordered assignment strategy based on path starting positions. \\ \hline

Regularization &
KL-divergence term enforcing a Gaussian prior on the latent space (VAE objective). \\ \hline

Optimization &
Adam optimizer with scheduled learning rate decay and gradient clipping. \\ \hline

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Overall Architecture &

Cross-attention maps from a frozen diffusion model are extracted to identify foreground objects and background. Attention-derived masks guide the separation of SVG primitives into semantic groups. \\ \hline

Attention Utilization &

Optimization Objective &
Rasterized SVG is iteratively optimized to match the text prompt using diffusion-based score distillation and additional regularization terms. \\ \hline

Guidance Models &

Optimization follows a score distillation principle, pushing SVG parameters toward high-probability regions of the text-conditioned image distribution. \\ \hline

LoRA Adaptation &
A lightweight LoRA module is trained to reshape diffusion gradients, improving stability when optimizing vector control points. \\ \hline

Reward Model &
A learned reward model evaluates visual plausibility and discourages artifacts such as oversmoothing or color saturation. \\ \hline

A weighted combination of diffusion score loss and reward-based regularization, backpropagated to SVG control points and colors. \\ \hline
